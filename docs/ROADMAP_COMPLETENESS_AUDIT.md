## Cross-cutting checks

- Persistence: most implemented systems have repositories/migrations and idempotent keys. Player lifestyle adaptation is backward-compatible through a defaulted migration column and transfer settlement reuses stable existing player/development identities. Career timeline projection uses stable source identities and only persisted evidence, so reload/read/role-switch retries cannot duplicate entries or invent history.
- Progression: match/season systems are real; funded federation policies advance at the season boundary, proposed policies remain inert, and election/policy completion events are stable inputs to the historical/media path. The national-development read model aggregates matching policy impacts, played senior/youth/women results, persisted academy pathway outcomes and cohort conversion without duplicating completed project effects. Recruitment decisions consume persisted knowledge confidence and trial progression before supported transfer choices. Manager vacancies now progress through deterministic discovery, interview, negotiation and appointment/fallback processing. Career timeline reads synchronize only real persisted milestones and career evidence.
- Roles: Manager, Owner/Chairman and President command paths are production-backed. Sporting Director/DoF and CEO/Secretary assignments now surface as active desktop roles and route covered commercial, budget, facility, club-finance, licensing, registration and staff-administration mutations through canonical authority. Agent has a persisted strategy, fee, reputation and timeline career path. Lifestyle effects are applied by the existing Manager training/transfer paths and do not grant Owner tactical authority.
- A/B/C: competition and founder paths use shared-root logic; no division-specific patch is warranted for this audit gap.
- External world: foreign clubs/leagues remain `CONTEXT_ONLY`; no full foreign executive, agent or media simulation is introduced.
- UI/release: Claude-owned presentation files and Apple signing are out of scope and unchanged.

Agent transfer/free-agent/renewal fees preserve the existing club expense path while crediting represented agents' personal wealth exactly once, and persisted client strategies provide bounded preference signals plus outcome/timeline updates. Continental coefficient progression is wired into completed continental season processing with real persisted Nepal fixture results and idempotent season snapshots. National development derives ten banded dimensions, capped policy impacts, bounded trends and explainable contributors from the existing federation profile and policy/project systems, plus played national-team results, women/girls programme signals and season-specific academy pathway conversion. Promise progression now adds explicit at-risk notifications and supported loan/squad-strengthening commitments while preserving legacy `KEPT` values.
