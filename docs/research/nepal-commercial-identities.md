## Lender identity seeds

The lender seed uses the Nepal Rastra Bank licensed-bank list as the authoritative identity source: [BFIs list, mid-January 2026](https://www.nrb.org.np/bfr/bfis-list-in-english-mid-january-2026/). The seeded names are Nepal Bank Limited, Nabil Bank Limited, Agriculture Development Bank Limited, Himalayan Bank Limited, and Muktinath Bikas Bank Limited. Terms, approval rules, rates, and repayment schedules are game simulation values and are not assertions about those institutions.
